get joke.

A small extension. Clear privacy.

Get Joke is made by MarkAlexI. It does not read your tabs, browsing history, or page content, and it has no accounts, advertising, or analytics.

Getting jokes

Jokes are requested directly from the third-party service JokeAPI. Requests include your chosen joke language, categories, and Safe mode filter. Like any web service, JokeAPI receives your IP address and standard network request information.

JokeAPI privacy policy

Requests omit cookies and referrer information.

Your settings

Settings use your browser’s extension storage and may sync through the browser when enabled. The last joke is stored locally and may be reused for 24 hours if the service is unavailable. It is replaced when a new joke loads and is not synced. Removing the extension clears local storage.

Safe mode

Safe mode is on by default. JokeAPI filters explicit content. Third-party filtering cannot guarantee that every joke will suit everyone.
