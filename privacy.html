get joke.

A small extension. Clear privacy.

Get Joke is made by MarkAlexI. It does not read your tabs, browsing history, or page content, and it has no accounts, advertising, or analytics.

Getting jokes

Jokes are requested directly from the third-party service JokeAPI. Requests include your chosen joke language, categories, and Safe mode filter. Like any web service, JokeAPI receives your IP address and standard network request information. Its own privacy policy applies. Requests omit cookies and referrer information.

Your settings

Settings are saved using your browser’s extension storage. If browser sync is enabled and supported, the browser may sync these preferences between your devices. The last joke is stored locally and may be reused for 24 hours if the service is unavailable. It is replaced when a new joke is loaded and is not synced to other devices. Removing the extension clears its local storage.

Safe mode

Safe mode is on by default. JokeAPI filters explicit content and dark jokes. Content is provided by a third party; filtering cannot guarantee that every joke will suit everyone.
